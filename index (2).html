The story

This is not therapy. This is not tourism.

Aksya Mundi is a threshold. We leave the fluorescent rooms and the crowded itineraries behind and let the mountain and the sea do their work, through story, breath, movement and ritual, in places where the world mirrors you back.

No guru. No saviour. Just a path, and support if you choose it.

Who this is for

The mountain tests you.

For the burnt out and the comfortably numb. For the ones tired of pretending, tired of hiding, tired of not being seen. Go as far as you can by yourself. Some paths are easier when witnessed.
